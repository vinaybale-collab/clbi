Takeaways

Soda ash, chlor-alkali and industrial-gas makers fill the bottom of the table. Tata Chemicals and DCM Shriram emit half of the group's Scope 1+2.

Takeaways

  • Climate intensity spans 153 times: 3.0 tCO2e per ₹ crore at UPL (standalone), 458.6 at Linde India. Energy spans 255 times, from 26 GJ (UPL) to 6,692 (PCBL, carbon black).
  • Tata Chemicals ranks last (3.0). It emits 28.0% of the group's Scope 1+2, uses 24.9% of its water and holds 47.2% of its unrecovered waste. It also returns 76% of the water it withdraws, the group's highest share. DCM Shriram (16th) adds 22.0% of emissions.
  • Godrej Industries draws 68.2% of its energy from renewables; the median is 7.4%. Paradeep Phosphates recovers all its waste and climbs from 15th on gross figures to 9th net. Solar Industries recovers 16% and drops from 3rd to 7th.
  • UPL ranks 1st but carries a naming caution: its standalone boundary covers a small part of a global group. Supreme Petrochem is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (79.3, 2nd). Climate and water intensity show almost no link (rank correlation 0.16), so water reorders the table.
  • Paradeep Phosphates describes its 2,280-acre site as 'a vital habitat for over 30,000 migratory birds from more than 50 species'. No other company in the dataset gives a clearer site-level biodiversity statement.

What better disclosure would change

  • Himadri Speciality Chemical stays unranked: it reports zero Scope 2 and no renewable energy, a combination it needs to correct.
  • CNPI holds back UPL's air figure (0.13% of the peer median). Four ranked companies do not report air in tonnes.
  • Land: FACT gives the one company-wide figure (607 ha). Tata Chemicals (Mithapur) and UPL (Ankleshwar Unit 3) answer 'yes' on sensitive areas but give no area or distance.

Caveats

  • The group joins agrochemical formulators (UPL, Sumitomo, PI) with fertiliser, soda ash, carbon black, industrial-gas and explosives makers, whose process intensities differ by orders of magnitude. CNPI could test a split: basic chemicals and fertilisers in one group, agrochemicals in another.
  • UPL's first place rests on a standalone BRSR covering ₹5,748 crore of revenue. Most of the group's operations sit outside that boundary.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 50.8% of EBITDA (n = 17), 6.4% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 64.6%; energy 8.5%; water 19.4%; waste 0.1%; air 3.2%. No rank uses these figures.
