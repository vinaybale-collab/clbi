Takeaways

Hotels and quick-service restaurants sit at the bottom. The top two, a distributor and a delivery platform, carry naming cautions, and the group spans 748 times on climate because it holds several industries.

Takeaways

  • The five hotel companies rank 13th to 16th and 19th of 19. The two quick-service restaurant chains rank 11th (Jubilant) and 18th (Devyani).
  • Climate intensity spans 748 times, from 0.045 tCO2e per ₹ crore (Redington) to 33.9 (Devyani). Water spans 1,426 times, up to Ventive Hospitality's 405 kL.
  • Redington (1st) reports climate, energy and water values 120 to 178 times below the median. Swiggy (2nd) runs an online platform; its delivery fleet and restaurants sit outside its Scope 1 and 2. Both carry naming cautions, so Lenskart is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (77.3, 3rd).
  • Avenue Supermarts (DMart) recovers none of its waste and holds 67.8% of the group's unrecovered waste. It also emits 18.5% of the group's Scope 1+2 and ranks 8th.
  • Scope 2 makes up a median 90% of emissions, yet no company passes half renewable energy (Chalet Hotels highest, 46%). Brainbees, Travel Food Services and Devyani reported water use as a flat 20% of withdrawal; CNPI scores them on withdrawal.

What better disclosure would change

  • CNPI holds back Eternal (Zomato) on all four pressures. Each value sits between 1/370 and 1/1,111 of the median, which points to a boundary problem.
  • Eight of 27 members stay unranked. ITC Hotels, Physicswallah and Urban Company report no pressures. Meesho reports zero Scope 1 and BLS International zero waste. CNPI holds back Metro Brands' waste figure, and International Gemological Institute has no matching revenue year.
  • Air: 11 of 19 report. Land: Chalet gives one partial figure; none of the 11 listed sites has coordinates.

Caveats

  • The group joins IT distribution, delivery platforms, grocery and apparel retail, quick-service restaurants and luxury hotels, which differ in kind.
  • Twelve report standalone and seven consolidated.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 2.7% of EBITDA (n = 23), 0.6% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 59.1%; energy 5.4%; water 35.2%; waste 0.1%; air 0%. No rank uses these figures.
