Takeaways

Hydro and renewable generators take the top five places per rupee. Per megawatt-hour, coal generators climb as many as five places.

Takeaways

  • Climate intensity spans 1,202 times, from 3.0 tCO2e per ₹ crore (SJVN) to 3,614 (Jaiprakash Power), the widest climate spread of any group.
  • Per megawatt-hour (rank correlation 0.85), NTPC climbs from 13th to joint 8th, Jaiprakash from 12th to 7th, Adani Power from 10th to 6th and JSW Energy from 9th to 5th. Scope 1+2 per MWh runs from 0.59 t (JSW Energy) to 1.31 t (Jaiprakash); NTPC sits at 0.88.
  • NTPC emits 59.1% of the group's Scope 1+2 and uses 57.5% of its water. With Adani Power and JSW Energy, three companies hold 82.5% of emissions.
  • Five ranked members carry naming cautions. SJVN (1st), Power Grid (3rd) and ACME Solar (4th) report values 100–300 times from the median. JSW Energy (9th) and Jaiprakash (12th) report Scope 1 above what their fuel could emit. NHPC is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (89.9, 2nd).
  • Returned water is the group's main offset: 8 of 13 return at least half of what they withdraw (median 56%; Tata Power highest at 92.8%). Larger companies rank worse (correlation between revenue and score −0.45).

What better disclosure would change

  • Fly ash: five coal generators (Adani Power, JSW Energy, NLC, Jaiprakash, Reliance Power) report waste 400 to 23,000 times the peer median; NTPC reports about 57,000 t. Generators need one common way to report ash.
  • Reliance Power stays unranked because it reports zero Scope 2. CNPI holds back Adani Energy Solutions' water figure and the SJVN and NHPC air figures.
  • Land: no company gives a land-held figure; ACME Solar's one statement covers acreage its team has acquired. No company makes a sensitive-area statement, though SJVN and NHPC run hydro projects in the Himalaya.

Caveats

  • Generators, a transmission company (Power Grid) and distribution-led businesses (CESC, Tata Power) share one table. The per-MWh view covers generators.
  • Eight report consolidated and five standalone.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 159.9% of EBITDA (n = 15), 38% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 66.6%; energy 8.5%; water 11.7%; waste 0%; air 5.9%. No rank uses these figures.
