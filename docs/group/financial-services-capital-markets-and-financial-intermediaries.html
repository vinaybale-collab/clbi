Takeaways

CNPI cannot yet rank a third of the group. Among the ranked, exchanges and depositories run data centres and carry most of the footprint; BSE ranks last.

Takeaways

  • Six of 18 members stay unranked. CNPI holds back two values (Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC's climate figure, Choice International's energy figure), three firms report zeros or gaps, and CRISIL reports on a calendar year.
  • BSE emits 29.7% of the group's Scope 1+2, holds 64.6% of its unrecovered waste and ranks 12th of 12. Its climate intensity (3.42 tCO2e per ₹ crore) is 88 times Angel One's (0.039).
  • The 12 ranked companies emit 51,323 tCO2e combined, about a tenth of Asahi India Glass's Scope 1+2. Scope 2 makes up a median 92% of the total.
  • Four members (BSE, HDFC AMC, JM Financial and the unranked Nuvama) reported water use as a flat 20% of withdrawal; CNPI scores them on withdrawal. JM Financial ranks 3rd and BSE last.
  • The median renewable share is 1.7%. 360 ONE WAM draws 70.8% and is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (76.7), up from 3rd on gross figures.

What better disclosure would change

  • Reported zeros and missing waste data keep three firms out (ICICI Prudential AMC, Anand Rathi Wealth, Nuvama). Choice International also carries a waste value 110 times below the median.
  • Firms should measure water discharge and stop assuming 80%.
  • Air: 2 of 12 report. All 18 members report office sites and no other land.

Caveats

  • Exchanges and depositories (BSE, CDSL, IEX) run data centres; brokers, asset managers and registrars run offices. One per-rupee ranking blends the two.
  • Eight report standalone and four consolidated.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 0.07% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 63.9%; energy 5.2%; water 30.3%; waste 0%; air 0%. No rank uses these figures.
