Takeaways

IT draws the highest renewable share of any group. Offsets reshape its ranking more than in any other group.

Takeaways

  • The median renewable share is 56.9%, and 11 of 20 pass half; Wipro draws 89.2%. Net and gross ranks correlate at 0.79, the lowest of the 24 groups.
  • Wipro climbs from 12th on gross figures to 4th net, Persistent from 6th to 1st and Infosys from 8th to 3rd. HCL Technologies drops from 4th to 9th and Oracle Financial Services from 5th to 10th.
  • Climate intensity spans 18 times (Indegene 0.12, Inventurus 2.23 tCO2e per ₹ crore). Larger firms rank higher (correlation between revenue and score 0.46).
  • Water runs against the other pressures (climate–water rank correlation −0.20). TCS uses 31.3% of the group's water and Infosys 22.4%.
  • Thirteen of 20 report air in tonnes, most of it from backup generators, and air rank shows no link to the main rank (0.17). Persistent (75.6) is the highest-ranked like-for-like company. Indegene (70.2) comes next: first on climate and energy, last on waste.

What better disclosure would change

  • CNPI holds back KPIT's energy figure (about 0.00003 GJ, a unit error). Pine Labs reports none of the pressures, Fractal Analytics reports no energy or waste, and Hexaware reports on a calendar year.
  • Land: Tech Mahindra gives the one company-wide figure (209 ha, 13 locations). TCS states that its Kalinga Park campus in Bhubaneswar, the ILDC host city, lies near the Chandaka Wildlife Sanctuary.
  • Scope 2 basis: with renewable shares this high, the market or location basis decides the climate score. Each company should state which it uses.

Caveats

  • Twelve report consolidated and eight standalone. The mix matters here: standalone boundaries leave out global delivery centres.
  • L&T Technology Services carries a 'discontinued operations: BRSR scope to confirm' flag. Paytm (One 97) and Black Box sit at the edge of the group's definition.
