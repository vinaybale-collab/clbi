Takeaways

The three gold jewellers take the top three places, and all three carry naming cautions. Crompton Greaves is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (joint 3rd, 66.7).

Takeaways

  • Titan (88.9), Thangamayil (86.1) and Kalyan Jewellers (66.7, joint 3rd) carry reviewed cautions: CNPI treats jewellery as unlike appliance and electronics making. Gold's high value lifts their per-rupee scores; revenue and score correlate at 0.59, second to insurers among the 24 groups.
  • Climate intensity spans 71 times: 0.32 tCO2e per ₹ crore at Titan, 23.0 at DOMS Industries, a stationery maker that also ranks last on energy.
  • Offsets add little. The median renewable share is 6.4% (Titan highest at 40.9%), 3 of 10 recover at least 90% of their waste, and no company moves three or more places from gross to net. Kalyan reported water use as a flat 20% of withdrawal, so CNPI scores it on withdrawal.
  • Voltas (22.0%), Amber (17.3%) and LG Electronics (15.1%) emit 54% of the group's Scope 1+2. Blue Star and PG Electroplast hold 65% of its unrecovered waste.
  • Voltas (8th) carries a naming caution. Its Scope 1 of 78,684 t equals 0.53 t per GJ of non-renewable energy; burning fuel gives at most about 0.1. Refrigerant leaks counted in Scope 1 would explain the gap, and Voltas should show them as a separate line.

What better disclosure would change

  • Air: 4 of 10 report in tonnes, too few for an air rank.
  • Voltas and the other air-conditioner makers should split Scope 1 into fuel and refrigerant (fugitive) emissions.
  • Land: no company gives a land figure. Companies list 7 sites, none with coordinates.

Caveats

  • CNPI compares gold jewellers with appliance assemblers, contract manufacturers (Amber, PG Electroplast) and a stationery maker (DOMS, from NSE FMCG). Per-rupee intensity favours the jewellers.
  • Eight report standalone and two consolidated.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 2.3% of EBITDA (n = 10), 0.2% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 67.3%; energy 5.2%; water 27.5%; waste 0.2%; air 0%. No rank uses these figures.
