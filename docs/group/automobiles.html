Takeaways

Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles is the highest-ranked like-for-like company and has the lowest climate, energy and water intensity. Maruti Suzuki emits 36% of the group's Scope 1+2 and ranks 8th of 12.

Takeaways

  • Spreads are narrow for manufacturers: 14 times on climate (1.02 tCO2e per ₹ crore at TMPV, 14.2 at Ola Electric), 7.8 times on water and 9.7 times on waste. The median is 2.17 tCO2e per ₹ crore.
  • Three companies emit 62.7% of the group's Scope 1+2: Maruti Suzuki 35.7%, Mahindra & Mahindra 17.7% and Tata Motors (commercial vehicles) 9.2%. Maruti uses 45.4% of the group's non-renewable energy.
  • Net and gross ranks correlate at 0.82, the second-lowest of the 24 groups. Ashok Leyland (56.8% renewable) climbs from 8th on gross figures to 5th net. Hero MotoCorp recovers 30% of its waste, the group's lowest, and drops from 3rd to 6th.
  • Eicher Motors draws 54% of its energy from renewables, yet ranks 11th of 12 on climate and mid-table on energy. That gap fits a Scope 2 figure calculated on grid averages, which ignores its renewable supply.
  • All 12 report air emissions in tonnes. Air rank has no link to the main rank (correlation −0.15): Hero MotoCorp ranks first on air, and Tata Motors (CV) has the highest air intensity.

What better disclosure would change

  • Each company should state its Scope 2 basis (location or market). Eicher shows how that choice moves the climate score.
  • CNPI withholds TVS Motor's rank because TVS reports recovering more than 1.5 times the waste it generated. Ather Energy reports none of the four pressures.
  • Land: no company gives a company-wide figure. Ten companies list 22 sites (2,102 ha); three give coordinates.

Caveats

  • The group sets cars, two-wheelers, commercial vehicles, tractors and an EV start-up side by side. Ola Electric's last place (4.5) reflects new plants set against ₹2,253 crore of consolidated revenue.
  • Escorts Kubota's rank carries a 'discontinued operations: BRSR scope to confirm' flag. Three members (tractors and CVs) came from NSE Capital Goods.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 1.2% of EBITDA (n = 11), 0.2% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 54.8%; energy 6.2%; water 32.7%; waste 0.2%; air 2.9%. No rank uses these figures.
