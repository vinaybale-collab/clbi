Takeaways

API makers fill the bottom of the table. The top three carry naming cautions, so GSK (4th) is the highest-ranked like-for-like drug maker.

Takeaways

  • Eight of the bottom nine (32nd to 40th) list APIs or fermentation as their main activity, among them Laurus, Divi's, Concord and Aurobindo. Energy intensity spans 868 times, from 1.6 GJ per ₹ crore (AstraZeneca) to 1,373 (Concord Biotech).
  • Five ranked members carry naming cautions. AstraZeneca (1st) imports and markets most of its medicines. Pfizer and Eris (joint 2nd) report Scope 1 of 39 t and 319 t, low enough to suggest outsourced manufacture. Cipla (15th) and Onesource (18th) report Scope 1 above what their fuel could emit. GlaxoSmithKline Pharmaceuticals is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (89.1, 4th).
  • The median renewable share is 36.7%, and 11 of 40 pass half. Granules climbs from 29th on gross figures to 17th net and Dr Reddy's from 15th to 6th. Anthem Biosciences recovers none of its waste and drops from 24th to 30th.
  • Aurobindo emits 22.2% of the group's Scope 1+2. Alembic (17.4%) and Lupin (16.5%) hold a third of its unrecovered waste.
  • Pharma has the most air reporters of any group (27 of 40, 26 air-ranked). Jubilant Pharmova ranks first on air.

What better disclosure would change

  • Water: the median company returns 6% of what it withdraws, and one returns more than half.
  • Land: Acutaas gives the one company-wide figure (9.3 ha). Lupin (23 ha), Natco (10.6 ha) and Mankind (9.2 ha) cover part of their companies. Laurus's 531.77 acres is a state allotment for its Pharma Zone. Every sensitive-area statement is a denial or a blank.
  • Thirteen ranked companies do not report air in tonnes.

Caveats

  • Importers, contract-development firms (Syngene, Sai, Anthem) and API makers run very different processes. CNPI could test a split between API and formulation makers.
  • The ranks contain five ties, at 2nd, 11th, 13th, 21st and 27th.
