Takeaways

Not ranked: the group has seven members. Grasim's cellulosic-fibre business emits 71% of the group's Scope 1+2.

Takeaways

  • Climate intensity spans 55 times (Page Industries 2.8, Grasim 154.9 tCO2e per ₹ crore). Trident has the highest water intensity (1,573 kL per ₹ crore) and holds 55% of unrecovered waste.
  • Four of seven return at least half of the water they withdraw (median 51%).

What better disclosure would change

  • Grasim's land figure (25,278 ha, FY2024-25) is a consolidated 25-site total that its subsidiary UltraTech assessed. Swan Corp carries a caution: its Scope 1 is more than its reported fuel could emit.

Caveats

  • Viscose-fibre production is chemical processing. Grasim came from NSE Construction Materials.
