Takeaways

Smelters drive this group's intensity. The three companies with aluminium smelters (Hindalco, NALCO, Vedanta) rank 7th to 9th and emit 94% of the ranked group's Scope 1+2.

Takeaways

  • Climate intensity spans 145 times, from 6.3 tCO2e per ₹ crore (NMDC, iron ore) to 910.9 (Vedanta). Water spans 192 times, from 27 kL per ₹ crore (Gravita) to 5,275 (Hindustan Copper).
  • Vedanta (61.8%), Hindalco (21.9%) and NALCO (10.0%) emit 93.8% of the ranked group's Scope 1+2 and use 93.7% of its non-renewable energy. Vedanta uses 57.3% of the group's water.
  • Offsets make no real difference. The median renewable share is 0.8% (Hindustan Zinc highest, 6.0%), and Scope 2 makes up a median 10% of emissions. Gross and net ranks correlate at 0.98 on the three scored pressures.
  • CNPI holds back Coal India: its Scope 2 (7.7 million t) implies more grid electricity than 1.5 times its total reported energy. Nine companies remain ranked, and NMDC is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (89.3).
  • This group gives the strongest land evidence of any industrial group. Vedanta reports 64,979 ha across 46 sites (FY2025-26 biodiversity assessment) and BCCL 16,390 ha (FY2024-25); Lloyds Metals' 348 ha covers one mining lease. None of the 12 listed sites carries a sensitive-area statement.

What better disclosure would change

  • Companies report overburden and tailings on such different bases that CNPI cannot score pollution. GMDC (69 t) and Jain Resource Recycling report waste about 1/10,000 to 1/600 of the median.
  • Coal India should reconcile its Scope 2 with its energy table. CNPI holds back Jain Resource Recycling's water figure; BCCL reports no climate, energy or water data, and Vedanta Aluminium Metal has no matching revenue year.
  • Air: 5 of 9 report in tonnes. None of the 12 sites has coordinates.

Caveats

  • Miners, smelters and a lead recycler share one table. Vedanta's rank carries a 'discontinued operations: BRSR scope to confirm' flag.
  • Seven report standalone and two consolidated (Vedanta, Hindustan Zinc).

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 30.4% of EBITDA (n = 10), 12.9% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 60.1%; energy 9%; water 16.3%; waste 4.6%; air 0.4%. No rank uses these figures.
