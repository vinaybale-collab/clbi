Takeaways

Insurers' climate intensity varies 3.1 times, the narrowest spread of any group, so water and waste decide the ranks. Larger insurers rank higher (revenue–score correlation 0.63, the strongest of any group).

Takeaways

  • Climate intensity runs from 0.10 tCO2e per ₹ crore (ICICI Prudential Life) to 0.31 (Star Health). Water spans 60 times and waste far more, so those two pressures sort the table.
  • LIC emits 64.5% of the group's Scope 1+2, uses 73.7% of its non-renewable energy and ranks 6th. CNPI measures its revenue as gross premium income (₹536,749 crore).
  • CNPI holds back Canara HSBC Life: its Scope 2 (1,765 tCO2e) is 8.6 times what its reported energy (1,037 GJ) could emit at the 0.71 t/MWh grid factor. Nine insurers remain ranked, one above the minimum of eight.
  • One of nine ranked insurers reports returning any water. Three report zero unrecovered waste, and none reports air in tonnes.
  • Star Health ranks last (4.8), lowest or second-lowest on every pressure. ICICI Prudential Life (78.8) is the highest-ranked like-for-like insurer, with SBI Life next (65.3).

What better disclosure would change

  • Canara HSBC Life should reconcile its energy and Scope 2 figures.
  • GIC Re and New India Assurance stay unranked because they report zero waste. Niva Bupa's sensitive-area answer repeats the BRSR question.
  • CNPI cannot compute a profit-based rank for this group.

Caveats

  • The group mixes life, general and health insurers. Premium income and investment-heavy life revenue measure different things.
  • CNPI covers operational footprints and leaves out insured and invested impacts.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 0.03% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 34.3%; energy 2.8%; water 62.9%; waste 0%; air 0%. No rank uses these figures.
