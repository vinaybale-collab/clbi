Takeaways

Banks' own footprints are small and close together. ICICI Bank is the highest-ranked like-for-like bank, and CNPI scores six banks that assumed 80% water discharge on withdrawal.

Takeaways

  • Climate intensity spans 34 times (Bandhan 0.09, Punjab & Sind 3.02 tCO2e per ₹ crore); the middle half sits between 1.02 and 1.65. Scope 2 makes up a median 94% of emissions. ICICI Bank draws the most renewable energy, at 29.7% (median 1.8%).
  • Six banks reported water use as a flat 20% of withdrawal, assuming 80% goes out as discharge. CNPI scores them on withdrawal: HDFC Bank (11th), Federal (6th), IndusInd (18th), Karur Vysya (22nd), RBL (4th) and Punjab & Sind (23rd).
  • Water and climate scores show no link (rank correlation −0.07). Bandhan ranks first on climate and energy, last on water (32.9 kL per ₹ crore), and 8th overall. Axis Bank has the lowest water intensity (1.33 kL) and ranks 3rd.
  • SBI emits 24.3% of the group's Scope 1+2 and ranks 10th. ICICI (84.8) is the highest-ranked like-for-like bank, ahead of Yes Bank (83.8) and Axis (74.0). Kotak returns 76% of its water and gains the most from offsets, climbing from 23rd on gross figures to 16th net.
  • Revenue-based and profit-based ranks correlate at 0.52, the second-lowest of any group. For banks, the choice of yardstick moves the result.

What better disclosure would change

  • Water: banks should meter discharge. Six applied a fixed 80% return instead, and CNPI scores them on withdrawal.
  • CNPI holds back Central Bank of India's energy figure (near zero) and Jammu & Kashmir Bank's water figure (1/769 of the median).
  • Banks do not report financed emissions or the land exposure of their loan books, so their main nature impact is missing. City Union Bank states that it runs branches in 'ecologically sensitive areas'.

Caveats

  • CNPI ranks operational footprints and leaves out financed impacts, so these ranks do not compare with real-economy sectors.
  • All 25 ranked banks report standalone.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 0.1% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 69%; energy 5.4%; water 24.8%; waste 0%; air 0%. No rank uses these figures.
