Takeaways

More than half the group draws at least half its energy from renewables. Offsets move more than half its members by three or more places.

Takeaways

  • The median renewable share is 54.7%, and 15 of 27 pass half; Cupid (99.4%) and United Spirits (94.2%) top the list. Net and gross ranks correlate at 0.85, and 15 of 27 companies move three or more places.
  • LT Foods (91.8% renewable) climbs from 16th on gross figures to 6th net, Nestlé India from 24th to 16th and United Spirits from 7th to 1st. Allied Blenders recovers none of its waste and drops from 11th to 20th; Cupid (₹358 crore revenue, no waste recovery) drops from 5th to 12th.
  • Hindustan Unilever (4th), United Breweries (13th) and Cupid (12th) rank on a zero Scope 2, which CNPI accepts because they report 96–100% renewable electricity. HUL has the group's lowest climate intensity (0.11 tCO2e per ₹ crore), which widens the climate spread to 954 times (E.I.D. Parry 104.4).
  • ITC emits 27.0% of the group's Scope 1+2 and ranks 23rd. It returns 63.5% of the water it withdraws, the group's highest share. Radico Khaitan recovers none of its waste and holds 46.8% of the group's unrecovered waste; United Breweries holds 18.1%.
  • Water spans 832 times (P&G Hygiene 1.85, E.I.D. Parry 1,538 kL per ₹ crore). E.I.D. Parry, a sugar company, ranks last. 22 of 27 ranked companies report air in tonnes.

What better disclosure would change

  • Three ranked companies carry naming cautions because their Scope 1 is more than their reported fuel could emit: Avanti Feeds (10th), Cupid (12th; 41.5 t per GJ of non-renewable energy) and Allied Blenders (20th; 0.75 t per GJ). Varun Beverages stays unranked because it reports on a calendar year.
  • Waste: Cupid, Radico Khaitan and Allied Blenders report no recovery, against a group median of 98%.
  • Land: no company gives a company-wide figure. HUL states that its Silvassa factory sits near the Dadra and Nagar Haveli Wildlife Sanctuary; Zydus Wellness states that forest surrounds its Sikkim plant.

Caveats

  • Agri-processors (sugar, edible oil, rice, aqua feed, dairy) sit beside branded personal-care and beverage makers. HUL ranks on a consolidated basis, with a 'discontinued operations: BRSR scope to confirm' flag.
  • Spirits and beer makers book state excise duty in revenue from operations, which enlarges the denominator. Read United Spirits' first place with that in mind.

The group’s median 4.4% of EBITDA (n = 27), 0.8% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 52.5%; energy 4.7%; water 39.1%; waste 0%; air 2.9%. No rank uses these figures.
