Takeaways

Cement shows the narrowest spread of any industrial group (2.7 times on climate). Small differences decide ranks, and switching from revenue to tonnes of cement reorders the middle of the table.

Takeaways

  • Climate intensity runs from 555 tCO2e per ₹ crore (JSW Cement) to 1,490 (India Cements), a 2.7-times range. The median of 1,020 is the highest of any peer group. Per tonne of cement the range shrinks to 1.46 times (0.46 to 0.67 tCO2e/t).
  • Measured per tonne (rank correlation 0.88), Nuvoco climbs from 6th to 4th and Ramco from 7th to 5th. Ambuja drops from 4th to 6th and Shree from 5th to 7th. Shree has the highest Scope 1+2 per tonne in the group (0.67).
  • Scope 1 makes up almost all of cement's climate pressure; Scope 2 is a median 3%. No company passes 7.5% renewable energy. Offsets move ranks half a place on average, and no company moves three or more.
  • Nine of 10 recover at least 90% of their waste through co-processing. Dalmia Bharat recovers 0.016% and holds 90.7% of the group's unrecovered waste.
  • ACC is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (86.1). UltraTech emits 42.9% of the group's Scope 1+2, uses 53.5% of its water and ranks 8th. All 10 report air in tonnes, and air rank tracks the main rank (correlation 0.88).

What better disclosure would change

  • Dalmia Bharat should check its waste-recovery figure against its co-processing disclosures.
  • Water: 2 of 10 report returning any water, against median consumption of 243 kL per ₹ crore.
  • Land: no company gives a company-wide figure, despite quarry-based operations. The 10 companies list 6 sites, and none reports mining-lease area in its BRSR.

Caveats

  • Six report standalone and four consolidated (UltraTech, Dalmia, JSW Cement, Nuvoco).
  • The per-tonne view uses cement production. Clinker share and blended-cement mix explain part of the spread.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 315.9% of EBITDA (n = 10), 62.6% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 88.9%; energy 5.8%; water 1.6%; waste 0%; air 4.2%. No rank uses these figures.
