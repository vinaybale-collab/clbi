Takeaways

Tyre, glass and forging makers fill the bottom of the table. Two tyre makers emit a third of the group's Scope 1+2.

Takeaways

  • Climate intensity spans 224 times: 0.46 tCO2e per ₹ crore at Bosch, 103.5 at Asahi India Glass. The median is 18.4; the middle half sits between 9.4 and 27.5.
  • The five tyre makers rank 17th to 25th of 27 (Apollo 17, Ceat 18, JK Tyre joint 19, MRF 23, Balkrishna 25). MRF (20.2%) and Balkrishna (14.2%) emit 34.4% of the group's Scope 1+2.
  • Bought electricity drives this group's climate score. Scope 2 makes up a median 78% of Scope 1+2 and passes half for 20 of 27 companies. Five companies draw over 50% of their energy from renewables (median 20.7%), with Bosch at 88% and ZF CV at 72%. Bosch ranks 4th (84.6); CNPI accepts its zero Scope 2 because it reports 100% renewable electricity.
  • JBM Auto holds 50.2% of the group's unrecovered waste. The median company recovers 95% of its waste, and 15 of 27 recover at least 90%. Happy Forgings recovers none and ranks last.
  • Tenneco Clean Air India is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (94.2), with ZF CV next (93.3). Both earn under ₹4,100 crore. Across the group, size has no link to score (rank correlation −0.01).

What better disclosure would change

  • Bombay Burmah (22nd) carries a naming caution. Its Scope 1 of 13,295 t works out to 0.33 t per GJ of non-renewable energy; burning fuel gives at most about 0.1.
  • Air: 16 of 27 ranked companies report NOx, SOx and PM in tonnes. CNPI holds back Motherson Sumi Wiring's air figure, about 1/10,000 of the peer median.
  • Land: no company reports a company-wide figure, and five give a partial or older one. Bombay Burmah describes its Singampatti estates as land 'classified as Tiger reserve under the Wildlife Protection Act'. That disclosure belongs in a land view.

Caveats

  • The group puts light assemblers (wiring harnesses, exhaust systems) beside process-heat businesses (tyres, glass, forgings, lead-acid batteries). Tyres and glass could form their own peer group if numbers allow.
  • Bombay Burmah earns ₹296 crore standalone, and its water use (503 kL per ₹ crore, the group maximum) fits plantation activity. CNPI should re-check its placement under auto components.
  • Schaeffler India and CIE Automotive stay unranked: CNPI found no FY2026 annual filing to match their BRSR year. 25 of the 27 ranked companies report standalone.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 9% of EBITDA (n = 27), 1.4% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 71%; energy 6.7%; water 20%; waste 0.1%; air 0%. No rank uses these figures.
