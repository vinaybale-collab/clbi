Takeaways

Defence and shipbuilding companies sit at the top; foundry, abrasive and wire-rope makers sit at the bottom. Two public-sector manufacturers use 78% of the group's water.

Takeaways

  • Energy intensity spans about 1,000 times: 1.0 GJ per ₹ crore at Bharat Electronics, 1,028 at Usha Martin. Climate spans 320 times (BEL 0.30, Usha Martin 94.4 tCO2e per ₹ crore). The median climate intensity is 5.9; the upper quartile starts at 19.1.
  • Zen Technologies is the highest-ranked like-for-like company (82.2, 1st, on ₹424 crore of revenue), with Cummins India next (77.3, 3rd). Bharat Electronics ranks 2nd (80.5) but carries a naming caution: its Scope 1 of 8,110 t works out to 0.29 t per GJ of non-renewable energy, more than its fuel could emit. Bharat Dynamics (7th) carries the same caution.
  • The bottom four (Tega, AIA Engineering, Carborundum Universal, Usha Martin) make metal or mineral consumables: wear parts, high-chrome castings, abrasives and steel wire rope. AIA holds 44.5% of the group's unrecovered waste.
  • HAL (44.9%) and BHEL (33.3%) use 78% of the group's net water and rank 14th and 21st. They also hold the largest company-wide land figures: BHEL 6,754 ha and HAL 4,860 ha (BEL 1,245 ha).
  • The median company recovers 38% of its waste, among the lowest of the scored groups. PTC Industries recovers all of it and climbs from 18th on gross figures to 12th net; HAL recovers 0.09%. Mazagon Dock ranks 4th (76.3); it reported water use as a flat 20% of withdrawal, so CNPI scores it on withdrawal.

What better disclosure would change

  • CNPI holds back three values. Ingersoll-Rand's Scope 2 of 5,017 tCO2e implies more grid electricity than 1.5 times its total reported energy. Thermax's energy figure is a unit error, and BEML reports water consumption more than 10 times withdrawal.
  • BEL and Bharat Dynamics should reconcile Scope 1 with their fuel tables. Azad Engineering reports a zero Scope 1 and stays unranked; SKF India (Industrial) reports no climate or energy, and KSB has no revenue matched to the BRSR year.
  • Air: 11 of 25 report. Land: three companies give company-wide figures and ten give partial or older ones. None of the 28 listed sites has coordinates.

Caveats

  • Foundry, abrasive and wire-rope businesses run like metals processors. Defence PSUs book large bought-out content in revenue, which lowers their per-rupee intensity.
  • All 25 ranked companies report standalone.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 3.5% of EBITDA (n = 28), 0.9% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 55%; energy 5.4%; water 30.7%; waste 0.4%; air 0%. No rank uses these figures.
