Takeaways

Paint and adhesive formulators take five of the top eight places. Fluorochemical and synthesis makers fill the bottom and emit close to two-thirds of the group's Scope 1+2.

Takeaways

  • Intensity splits into two clusters. Climate runs from 1.7 tCO2e per ₹ crore (JSW Dulux) to 157.1 (Gujarat Fluorochemicals), 93 times, and the middle half spans 3.7 to 108.1.
  • SRF (28.8%), Aarti Industries (19.5%) and Gujarat Fluorochemicals (15.4%) emit 63.6% of the group's Scope 1+2 and rank 13th, 12th and 15th. SRF and Navin Fluorine hold 53.7% of its unrecovered waste.
  • JSW Dulux, Kansai Nerolac, Pidilite, Asian Paints and Berger all rank in the top eight. JSW Dulux (85.7) and BASF India (84.6) are the highest-ranked like-for-like companies.
  • Pidilite (53% renewable, 45% of water returned) climbs from 8th on gross figures to 5th net. Berger recovers 9.6% of its waste and drops from 4th to 8th.
  • 13 of 15 report air in tonnes. Vinati Organics has the highest air intensity (0.73 t per ₹ crore).

What better disclosure would change

  • CNPI holds back PRIVI Speciality on two counts: it reports water consumed above 10 times water withdrawn, and waste recovered above 1.5 times waste generated.
  • Vinati Organics (10th) carries a naming caution: its Scope 1 of 311,643 t is more than its reported fuel could emit. CNPI holds back 3M India's air figure (0.1% of the median), and Castrol stays unranked because it reports on a calendar year.
  • Land: no company gives a company-wide figure. 8 companies list 9 sites, and one gives coordinates.

Caveats

  • Paint makers blend and fill; fluorochemical and specialty-synthesis makers run reactions, which use far more energy per rupee. 3M India reports that 41% of its revenue is traded goods.
  • Four members, the paint makers, came from NSE Consumer Durables.

Illustrative nature cost

The group’s median 11.2% of EBITDA (n = 16), 2.9% of revenue. Median company shares by pressure: carbon 67.5%; energy 7.5%; water 20.2%; waste 0.2%; air 1%. No rank uses these figures.
