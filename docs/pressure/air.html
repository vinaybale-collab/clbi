Key points

  • The binding constraint is the unit: 111 companies report air values that cannot be converted to tonnes.
  • Among coal generators reported tonnes are internally consistent (SOx 3.0-5.0 kg/tCO2): air is credible where measured continuously and reported as mass.
  • Ten companies with more than 1 Mt Scope 1 each (incl. NLC India 31.6 Mt, Coal India 28.2 Mt) report no air tonnage.
  • The ladder spans about 600,000x (Power 7.7 to IT services 0.000013 t/Rs cr); only within-group air ranks are defensible.

Reporting practice, contradictions and limits

What BRSR asks. Essential Indicator 6 asks for NOx, SOx, particulate matter, persistent organic pollutants, volatile organic compounds, hazardous air pollutants and "others". Against each, the company must "please specify unit". The form does not require tonnes.

What companies report.

  • Coverage. 245 of 500 companies have all three of NOx, SOx and PM in tonnes. 178 of them are ranked on air, in 14 groups.
  • Unit exclusions. A further 111 companies report values in units that are not annual mass (concentrations, rates, or undeclared units), and these were excluded. The parser marks 170–173 filings per pollutant as "unit unresolved".
  • Other pollutants. In the 57-company subset, 41 have some POP, VOC or HAP entry.
  • Context. IIM Bangalore finds that 29.3% of the top 1,000 listed companies report no air data at all for FY2024-25 (Tripathi et al. 2026).

Boundary conditions that change the number.

  • Mass vs concentration. A stack concentration (mg/Nm³) cannot be turned into tonnes without the flow and operating hours, so most of the unit-unresolved filings cannot be used at all.
  • Which sources. It is not stated whether figures cover captive power plants, diesel generator sets and fugitive dust (mines, yards).
  • Reporting rate by group. The share of companies reporting tonnes is:
  • Cement 100% and Automobiles 93%;
  • Construction 15%;
  • Banks and Capital markets 11%;
  • Insurance 8%.

Contradictions (computed).

  • Large emitters with no air tonnage. Ten companies with more than 1 Mt of Scope 1 each report no NOx, SOx and PM in tonnes: NLC India (31.6 Mt), Coal India (28.2 Mt), Shyam Metalics (12.0 Mt), National Aluminium (11.5 Mt), Sarda Energy (8.5 Mt), NAVA (3.3 Mt), Gallantt Ispat (2.1 Mt), Jindal Saw (1.6 Mt), Chambal Fertilisers (1.5 Mt) and Oil India (1.2 Mt).
  • Reported zero air with real combustion. Kajaria Ceramics reports zero air pollutants with 414,938 t of Scope 1. Eicher Motors reports zero with 21,959 t.
  • Coal power is coherent. For the eight coal-heavy generators reporting tonnes, SOx per tonne of Scope 1 CO2 lies within 3.0–5.0 kg, NOx within 0.8–1.7 kg and PM within 0.14–0.24 kg. These are plausible and mutually consistent ratios.
  • Cement is not coherent. SOx per tonne of Scope 1 ranges 60-fold, from Shree Cement (0.005 kg) to UltraTech (0.31 kg). NOx per tonne ranges ninefold, from JSW Cement (0.41 kg) to India Cements (3.69 kg). Some of this is real (fuel and raw-mix sulphur), but a ninefold spread in NOx per tonne of clinker-related CO2 suggests different measurement bases.

What the number can and cannot support.

  • It can rank Power and the heavy-industry groups where reporting is near-universal and ratios are coherent.
  • It cannot:
  • rank groups where most companies do not report;
  • say anything about exposure: tonnes emitted near a city differ from tonnes emitted in a remote area;
  • be extended to VOCs and other pollutants without a unit rule.

Insights.

  1. The binding constraint is the unit, not the will to report: 111 companies report air values that cannot be converted to tonnes. BRSR's "please specify unit" invites this.
  2. Among coal generators the reported tonnes are internally consistent (SOx 3.0–5.0 kg per tonne of CO2). Air is credible where it is measured continuously and reported as mass.
  3. Ten companies with more than 1 Mt of Scope 1 each report no air tonnage, including NLC India (31.6 Mt) and Coal India (28.2 Mt), and Kajaria Ceramics reports zero. Coverage is weakest exactly where the pressure should be largest outside Power.
  4. The ladder spans about 600,000× (Power 7.7 t/₹ cr; IT services 0.000013), so a separate air rank within groups, as in CNPI, is the only defensible use.
