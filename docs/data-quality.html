3

Zeros and held-back values

For an operating company, a reported zero tends to mean “not measured”. We keep it and leave it unscored until the company confirms it. We hold a value back if its gross figure sits more than 300 times above or below the peer median per rupee, if its tables disagree (water consumed more than 10 times withdrawn; waste recovered more than 1.5 times generated), or if its Scope 2 implies an electricity bill larger than revenue.

5

Analysis: disclosure quality, the funnel and data quality by parameter

Draft for internal use, not for publication. Built from the final CNPI v1.2 outputs (outputs_v12/, build of 2026-10-28 12:01:19 UTC).

Scope. The universe is India's 500 largest NSE companies, and the reporting year is FY2025-26. This note looks at the data, not the scores. It asks four questions: - How many companies get from "in the universe" to "ranked", and where do the others drop out? - For each environmental parameter, how complete, how machine-readable and how internally consistent is what companies report? - Where do peer groups differ? - What do the final quality rules do?

Framing. The approach borrows from the IIM Bangalore BRSR study: coverage, non-reporting rates, quality tiers and sector contrasts. That study is Tripathi, Gupta, Krishna and Gandhi (2026), Business responsibility and sustainability in India: a comprehensive analysis of BRSR disclosures of India's top 1,000 listed companies for FY 2024–25, IIM Bangalore monograph, https://research.iimb.ac.in/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1149&context=monographs ("IIMB"). IIMB covers 982 companies in FY2024-25. CNPI covers 500 companies in FY2025-26 and uses a stricter test of "usable". The comparisons are therefore indicative, not like-for-like.

Files in this folder: - funnel.csv holds two tables: - company_waterfall: each company is assigned to the first stage it fails; - parameter_funnel: for each parameter, the count reported, then usable, then scored. - param_quality.csv holds quality per metric and per parameter: source-tier mix, flags, boundary, unit fixes and held-back values. Supplementary rows cover: - Scope 2 basis and Scope 3; - physical-output intensity; - air unit problems; - land categories and the classified sensitive-area statements.

It also holds rule rows giving how many companies each final rule affects. - coverage_by_group.csv is the heat table. Its rows are 29 peer groups and 20 NSE sectors. For each parameter it gives % reported and % usable. It also gives % complete, % ranked, land figures, and held-back values for the four pressures and for air separately.

2. Data quality by parameter

2.1 Source tiers

The tiers come from the source_status column of CLBI_v1_company_data.csv: - T1: XBRL value with an explicit unit, for the current entity and period. - T2: XBRL value whose unit was reconciled against the column in the filed BRSR PDF. - T3: XBRL value with a unit rule applied. Rule R1 reads "MtCO2e" as tonnes; rule R2 reads t/yr or kg/yr as an annual mass. - T4: value corrected after checking the filed BRSR PDF. - T5: value gap-filled from a public report and quoted verbatim. - X: excluded, either because the period is not aligned or because the value is unresolved or unavailable.

6. Method notes

Usable. A value is usable when four conditions hold: - the company reported it; - its gross figure is non-zero (for climate, neither Scope 1 nor Scope 2 is zero, unless a zero Scope 2 is accepted under the ≥ 90% renewables rule); - it is not held back; - its revenue is matched to the BRSR year.

Equivalently, {e}_per_rev is not null in the scores file.

Required pressures. These are climate, energy, water and pollution. Pollution is dropped in the four groups listed in build_stats.json under params.pollution_not_comparable; the script reads that list directly.

XBRL counts. These use the 465 filings in normalized_companies.json. "Fact present" means every component has status reported or unit_unresolved. "Unit resolved" means every component is reported. The components are: - climate: Scope 1 and Scope 2; - energy: total and non-renewable; - water: withdrawal and consumption; - pollution: waste generated and recovered; - air: NOx, SOx and PM.

Boundary. At metric level this is company_data reporting_boundary, bucketed by its leading word. At company level it is the scores file brsr_reporting_boundary.

Unit conversion. An accepted QA row counts as converted when its standard-unit value differs from the first number printed (relative tolerance 1e-6).

Land. Land is evidence only. The categories (A, R, B, C, D, E) are hand-classified in the final CLBI_v1_land_evidence.csv.

IIMB citations. Chapter numbers follow IIMB §2.6, under which Chapters 3–9 cover E1–E7. Page numbers are page markers in the extracted PDF text, retrieved 28 Oct 2026 with the Parallel web_fetch tool, and should be checked against the printed PDF. All IIMB figures are for FY2024-25 and 982 companies.

Data queries. The 53 rows in CLBI_v1_data_queries.csv (42 excluded from score, 11 shown only) overlap with the zero flags for 2 climate and 2 water values.
