How to read this page
  • Intensity is the reported quantity divided by FY2025-26 revenue in ₹ crore. Lower is better.
  • Element scores (0–100) place each company among the peers that reported that element: the lowest intensity scores 100, the highest 0. Only the order matters, so one extreme value cannot distort the others.
  • Pressure score is the average of climate, water and waste; all three must be reported. Credit for restoration, recycling and renewable electricity adds up to 10 points. Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score.
  • A high rank means only that the company reported lower pressure per rupee than most peers. It is not a biodiversity or ESG rating.

How each final score is built

Pressure score plus the three credits, for every company with a pressure score. Credit is deliberately small: it recognises effort, it does not offset pressure.

Intensity per ₹ crore of revenue

Coloured bars are ranked companies; pale bars are scored but not ranked; grey bars are reported but not scored (reported zero, open data query, or group not scored). A logarithmic axis is used where values span more than a hundredfold.

Illustrative nature cost

Illustrative — not a liability, not used in rank. Reported quantities multiplied by central prices (carbon at the global social cost, other pressures India-adjusted), as a share of revenue. Each dot is a company; the vertical line is the group median (shown only where at least 5 companies have a value). Read it as a sector pattern, not a precise company figure.
