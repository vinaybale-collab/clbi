Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 6,17,113 + Scope 2 2,89,920 = 9,07,033 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity9,07,033 ÷ ₹1,53,680.17 crore = 5.902 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 7) ÷ (11 − 1) = 40.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 1,74,61,322 m³
  2. Intensity1,74,61,322 ÷ ₹1,53,680.17 crore = 113.6 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 7) ÷ (11 − 1) = 40.0

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 5,96,194 tonnes
  2. Intensity5,96,194 ÷ ₹1,53,680.17 crore = 3.879 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 8 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (8 − 5) ÷ (8 − 1) = 42.9

  1. QuantityNOx 18.08 + SOx 8.57 + PM 15.63 = 42.28 tonnes
  2. Intensity42.28 ÷ ₹1,53,680.17 crore = 0.0002751 tonnes per ₹ crore
