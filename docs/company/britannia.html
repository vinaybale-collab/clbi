Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 89,855 + Scope 2 68,961 = 1,58,816 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,58,816 ÷ ₹19,151.59 crore = 8.293 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position14 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 14) ÷ (26 − 1) = 48.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 10,37,489 m³
  2. Intensity10,37,489 ÷ ₹19,151.59 crore = 54.17 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 29 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (29 − 12) ÷ (29 − 1) = 60.7

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 22,588.28 tonnes
  2. Intensity22,588.28 ÷ ₹19,151.59 crore = 1.179 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position13 of 29 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (29 − 13) ÷ (29 − 1) = 57.1

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(48.0 + 60.7 + 57.1) ÷ 3 = 55.3
  2. RestorationNo qualifying restoration = +0.0
  3. Recycling22,562.23 recovered ÷ 22,588.28 generated = 0.999; 3 × 0.999 = +3.00
  4. Renewables2 × 0.347 (renewable share 34.7%) = +0.69

  6. Finalmin(100, 55.3 + 3.69) = 59.0
  7. RankRank 12 of 26 by final score in Fast Moving Consumer Goods; without credit it would rank 11.

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+3.00

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 99.9% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.69

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 34.7% renewable as reported.
