Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 1,28,794.15 + Scope 2 87,734.01 = 2,16,528.16 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity2,16,528.16 ÷ ₹2,639.46 crore = 82.04 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 15) ÷ (26 − 1) = 44.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 12,09,161 m³
  2. Intensity12,09,161 ÷ ₹2,639.46 crore = 458.1 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position17 of 27 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (27 − 17) ÷ (27 − 1) = 38.5

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 67,822.714 tonnes
  2. Intensity67,822.714 ÷ ₹2,639.46 crore = 25.7 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position21 of 27 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (27 − 21) ÷ (27 − 1) = 23.1

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 611 + SOx 137 + PM 98 = 846 tonnes
  2. Intensity846 ÷ ₹2,639.46 crore = 0.3205 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position18 of 21 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (21 − 18) ÷ (21 − 1) = 15.0

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.57

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 85.7% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.12

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 6.1% renewable as reported.
