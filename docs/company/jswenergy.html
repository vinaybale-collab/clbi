Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 3,20,85,981.42 + Scope 2 43,205.83 = 3,21,29,187.25 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity3,21,29,187.25 ÷ ₹18,901.13 crore = 1,700 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 11) ÷ (15 − 1) = 28.6

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 15,63,14,699.23 m³
  2. Intensity15,63,14,699.23 ÷ ₹18,901.13 crore = 8,270 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 10) ÷ (15 − 1) = 35.7

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 59,78,975.526 tonnes
  2. Intensity59,78,975.526 ÷ ₹18,901.13 crore = 316.3 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 12) ÷ (14 − 1) = 15.4

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 45,760.75 + SOx 98,663.36 + PM 4,536.17 = 1,48,960.28 tonnes
  2. Intensity1,48,960.28 ÷ ₹18,901.13 crore = 7.881 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 8 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.89

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 96.3% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+1.51

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 75.4% renewable as reported.
