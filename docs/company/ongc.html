Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 83,79,783.82 + Scope 2 4,31,535.83 = 88,11,319.65 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity88,11,319.65 ÷ ₹1,32,508.14 crore = 66.5 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position13 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 13) ÷ (15 − 1) = 14.3

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 17,04,19,344.18 m³
  2. Intensity17,04,19,344.18 ÷ ₹1,32,508.14 crore = 1,286 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position14 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 14) ÷ (15 − 1) = 7.1

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 19,743.47 tonnes
  2. Intensity19,743.47 ÷ ₹1,32,508.14 crore = 0.149 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position8 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 8) ÷ (14 − 1) = 46.2

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 2,625.93 + SOx 858.83 + PM 1,375.78 = 4,860.54 tonnes
  2. Intensity4,860.54 ÷ ₹1,32,508.14 crore = 0.03668 tonnes per ₹ crore

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. A project was reviewed (Goa Green Credit eco restoration, In progress not completed) but did not qualify. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+0.03

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 1.0% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.30

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 15.1% renewable as reported.
