Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 203.46 + Scope 2 8,205.83 = 8,409.29 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity8,409.29 ÷ ₹9,373.97 crore = 0.8971 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 13 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (13 − 6) ÷ (13 − 1) = 58.3

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 99,418.03 m³
  2. Intensity99,418.03 ÷ ₹9,373.97 crore = 10.61 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 16 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (16 − 12) ÷ (16 − 1) = 26.7

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 32.28 tonnes
  2. Intensity32.28 ÷ ₹9,373.97 crore = 0.003444 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position4 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 4) ÷ (14 − 1) = 76.9

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 0.02 + SOx 0.01 + PM 0 = 0.03 tonnes
  2. Intensity0.03 ÷ ₹9,373.97 crore = 3.2 × 10-6 tonnes per ₹ crore
