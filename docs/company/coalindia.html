Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 2,81,90,235 + Scope 2 77,05,014.77 = 3,58,95,249.77 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity3,58,95,249.77 ÷ ₹1,68,400.29 crore = 213.2 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 15) ÷ (15 − 1) = 0.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 64,33,96,000 m³
  2. Intensity64,33,96,000 ÷ ₹1,68,400.29 crore = 3,821 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 15) ÷ (15 − 1) = 0.0
