Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 98.64 + Scope 2 20,725.2 = 20,823.84 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity20,823.84 ÷ ₹5,214.86 crore = 3.993 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 20 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (20 − 7) ÷ (20 − 1) = 68.4

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 8,85,983.84 m³
  2. Intensity8,85,983.84 ÷ ₹5,214.86 crore = 169.9 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position16 of 23 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (23 − 16) ÷ (23 − 1) = 31.8

  3. Position5 of 20 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (20 − 5) ÷ (20 − 1) = 78.9
