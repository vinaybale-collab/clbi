Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 3,56,30,000 + Scope 2 6,90,000 = 3,63,20,000 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity3,63,20,000 ÷ ₹54,023.14 crore = 672.3 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position17 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 17) ÷ (19 − 1) = 11.1

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 7,54,20,000 m³
  2. Intensity7,54,20,000 ÷ ₹54,023.14 crore = 1,396 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position13 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 13) ÷ (18 − 1) = 29.4

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 1,29,49,507.48 tonnes

  3. Position12 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 12) ÷ (18 − 1) = 35.3
