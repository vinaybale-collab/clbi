Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 5,466 + Scope 2 32,899 = 38,365 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity38,365 ÷ ₹14,807.41 crore = 2.591 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 3) ÷ (11 − 1) = 80.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 26,73,373 m³
  2. Intensity26,73,373 ÷ ₹14,807.41 crore = 180.5 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 12 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (12 − 11) ÷ (12 − 1) = 9.1

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 16,198.61 tonnes
  2. Intensity16,198.61 ÷ ₹14,807.41 crore = 1.094 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position9 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 9) ÷ (11 − 1) = 20.0

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 0.485 + SOx 0.184 + PM 0.114 = 0.783 tonnes
  2. Intensitynot computed: revenue not matched to the BRSR year
  3. Scorenot scored: open data query (see below)

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

Recycling+3.00
