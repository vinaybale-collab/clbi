How the score is calculated

Final score = average of the pressure scores. Each pressure score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where position ranks the company’s net intensity among the n peers with a scored value (1 = lowest), and net intensity = (gross − offset) ÷ revenue.

Net pressureNp = Gp − Op(1)
Net intensityIp = Gp − OpR(2)
Pressure scoresp = 100 × np − rpnp − 1(3)
Final scoreS = 13 Σp sp(4)

Final score = (0.0 + 0.0 + 0.0) ÷ 3 = 0.0; the 12th highest of 12 final scores in Construction and engineering, so peer rank 12.
