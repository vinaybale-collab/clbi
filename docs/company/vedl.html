Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 6,82,18,241 + Scope 2 32,30,048 = 7,14,48,289 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity7,14,48,289 ÷ ₹78,437 crore = 910.9 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position18 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 18) ÷ (19 − 1) = 5.6

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 23,31,78,540 m³
  2. Intensity23,31,78,540 ÷ ₹78,437 crore = 2,973 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 15) ÷ (18 − 1) = 17.6

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 14,96,77,705 tonnes
  2. Intensity14,96,77,705 ÷ ₹78,437 crore = 1,908 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position17 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 17) ÷ (18 − 1) = 5.9

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 1,15,992 + SOx 4,21,680 + PM 15,534 = 5,53,206 tonnes
  2. Intensity5,53,206 ÷ ₹78,437 crore = 7.053 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 12 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (12 − 12) ÷ (12 − 1) = 0.0

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+0.96

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 32.2% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.85

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 42.3% renewable as reported.
