Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 1,37,749.39 + Scope 2 5,128.84 = 1,42,878.23 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,42,878.23 ÷ ₹6,140.88 crore = 23.27 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 7) ÷ (11 − 1) = 40.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 1,93,350.94 m³
  2. Intensity1,93,350.94 ÷ ₹6,140.88 crore = 31.49 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 12 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (12 − 6) ÷ (12 − 1) = 54.5

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 366.66 tonnes
  2. Intensity366.66 ÷ ₹6,140.88 crore = 0.05971 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position4 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 4) ÷ (11 − 1) = 70.0
