Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 83.85 + Scope 2 9,671.57 = 9,755.42 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity9,755.42 ÷ ₹14,748.45 crore = 0.6615 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position8 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 8) ÷ (19 − 1) = 61.1

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 86,607.64 m³
  2. Intensity86,607.64 ÷ ₹14,748.45 crore = 5.872 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 5) ÷ (18 − 1) = 76.5

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 754.15 tonnes
  2. Intensity754.15 ÷ ₹14,748.45 crore = 0.05113 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position14 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 14) ÷ (18 − 1) = 23.5

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 0.00798 + SOx 0.00973 + PM 0.013 = 0.031 tonnes
  2. Intensity0.031 ÷ ₹14,748.45 crore = 2.09 × 10-6 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position1 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 1) ÷ (11 − 1) = 100.0

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.89

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 96.2% recovered as reported.
