Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 2,20,31,434.96 + Scope 2 3,13,671.53 = 2,23,45,106.49 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity2,23,45,106.49 ÷ ₹19,310.52 crore = 1,157 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position9 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 9) ÷ (11 − 1) = 20.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 27,89,401.48 m³
  2. Intensity27,89,401.48 ÷ ₹19,310.52 crore = 144.4 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position1 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 1) ÷ (11 − 1) = 100.0

  3. Position2 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 2) ÷ (11 − 1) = 90.0

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+3.00

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 100.0% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+1.24

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 62.0% renewable as reported.
