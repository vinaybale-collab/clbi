Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 6,40,00,000 + Scope 2 50,00,000 = 6,90,00,000 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity6,90,00,000 ÷ ₹1,39,720.22 crore = 493.8 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position13 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 13) ÷ (19 − 1) = 33.3

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 11,06,22,895.53 m³
  2. Intensity11,06,22,895.53 ÷ ₹1,39,720.22 crore = 791.7 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position8 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 8) ÷ (18 − 1) = 58.8

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 1,87,82,249 tonnes
  2. Intensity1,87,82,249 ÷ ₹1,39,720.22 crore = 134.4 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 10) ÷ (18 − 1) = 47.1

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 27,000 + SOx 67,000 + PM 9,000 = 1,03,000 tonnes
  2. Intensity1,03,000 ÷ ₹1,39,720.22 crore = 0.7372 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position8 of 12 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (12 − 8) ÷ (12 − 1) = 36.4

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. A project was reviewed (Port Talbot habitat creation permit, Commitment not achieved) but did not qualify. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+3.00

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 100.0% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.09

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 4.6% renewable as reported.
