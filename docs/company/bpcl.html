Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 95,95,518.89 + Scope 2 8,70,863.01 = 1,04,66,381.9 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,04,66,381.9 ÷ ₹5,22,668.25 crore = 20.02 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 5) ÷ (15 − 1) = 71.4

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 5,09,09,250 m³
  2. Intensity5,09,09,250 ÷ ₹5,22,668.25 crore = 97.4 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 6) ÷ (15 − 1) = 64.3

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 1,67,080.46 tonnes
  2. Intensity1,67,080.46 ÷ ₹5,22,668.25 crore = 0.3197 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 11) ÷ (14 − 1) = 23.1

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 6,184.83 + SOx 8,214.86 + PM 339.28 = 14,738.97 tonnes
  2. Intensity14,738.97 ÷ ₹5,22,668.25 crore = 0.0282 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 8 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (8 − 3) ÷ (8 − 1) = 71.4

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score.
