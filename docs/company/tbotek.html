Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 28.33 + Scope 2 757.47 = 785.8 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity785.8 ÷ ₹583.83 crore = 1.346 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 20 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (20 − 3) ÷ (20 − 1) = 89.5

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 15,046.65 m³
  2. Intensity15,046.65 ÷ ₹583.83 crore = 25.77 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 23 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (23 − 12) ÷ (23 − 1) = 50.0

  3. Position2 of 20 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. A project was reviewed (TBO Cares community plantation, Qualitative annual plantation) but did not qualify. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.
