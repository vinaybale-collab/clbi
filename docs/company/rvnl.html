Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 78.76 + Scope 2 12,583.26 = 12,662.02 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity12,662.02 ÷ ₹20,012.26 crore = 0.6327 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 3) ÷ (11 − 1) = 80.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 25,661.25 m³
  2. Intensity25,661.25 ÷ ₹20,012.26 crore = 1.282 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position1 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 1) ÷ (11 − 1) = 100.0

  2. Intensitynot computed: revenue not matched to the BRSR year
  3. Scorenot scored: open data query (see below)

From element scores to rank

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. A project was reviewed (Rail corridor afforestation wetland, In progress not completed) but did not qualify. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.98

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 99.3% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.01

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 0.3% renewable as reported.
