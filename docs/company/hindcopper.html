Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 9,653.08 + Scope 2 1,10,911 = 1,20,564.08 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,20,564.08 ÷ ₹3,077.92 crore = 39.17 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 5) ÷ (19 − 1) = 77.8

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 1,64,77,712.19 m³
  2. Intensity1,64,77,712.19 ÷ ₹3,077.92 crore = 5,354 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position18 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 18) ÷ (18 − 1) = 0.0

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 35,53,259.361 tonnes
  2. Intensity35,53,259.361 ÷ ₹3,077.92 crore = 1,154 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 15) ÷ (18 − 1) = 17.6

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.
