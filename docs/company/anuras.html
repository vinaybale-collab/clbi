Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 1,04,576 + Scope 2 24,010 = 1,28,586 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,28,586 ÷ ₹2,365.45 crore = 54.36 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 11) ÷ (26 − 1) = 60.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 5,43,638 m³
  2. Intensity5,43,638 ÷ ₹2,365.45 crore = 229.8 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position9 of 27 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (27 − 9) ÷ (27 − 1) = 69.2

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 32,597.01 tonnes
  2. Intensity32,597.01 ÷ ₹2,365.45 crore = 13.78 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position18 of 27 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (27 − 18) ÷ (27 − 1) = 34.6

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 56 + SOx 44 + PM 30 = 130 tonnes
  2. Intensity130 ÷ ₹2,365.45 crore = 0.05496 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 21 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (21 − 12) ÷ (21 − 1) = 45.0

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score. Air rank: 12 of 21.

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(60.0 + 69.2 + 34.6) ÷ 3 = 54.6
  2. RestorationNo qualifying restoration = +0.0
  3. Recycling32,004 recovered ÷ 32,597.01 generated = 0.982; 3 × 0.982 = +2.95
  4. Renewables2 × 0.354 (renewable share 35.4%) = +0.71
  5. Credit+3.65 (capped at 10)
  6. Finalmin(100, 54.6 + 3.65) = 58.3
  7. RankRank 11 of 26 by final score in Chemicals; without credit it would rank 11.

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.95

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 98.2% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.71

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 35.4% renewable as reported.
