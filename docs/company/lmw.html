Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 3,966.9 + Scope 2 1,422.8 = 5,389.7 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity5,389.7 ÷ ₹3,081.84 crore = 1.749 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 56 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (56 − 7) ÷ (56 − 1) = 89.1

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 3,18,033 m³
  2. Intensity3,18,033 ÷ ₹3,081.84 crore = 103.2 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position46 of 61 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (61 − 46) ÷ (61 − 1) = 25.0

  3. Position39 of 61 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (61 − 39) ÷ (61 − 1) = 36.7

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+3.00

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 100.0% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+1.95

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 97.6% renewable as reported.
