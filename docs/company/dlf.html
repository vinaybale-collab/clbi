Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 27,728.57 + Scope 2 1,63,181.34 = 1,90,909.91 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,90,909.91 ÷ ₹8,194.02 crore = 23.3 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position9 of 9 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (9 − 9) ÷ (9 − 1) = 0.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 71,51,087.23 m³
  2. Intensity71,51,087.23 ÷ ₹8,194.02 crore = 872.7 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 10) ÷ (11 − 1) = 10.0

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 2,36,461.93 tonnes
  2. Intensity2,36,461.93 ÷ ₹8,194.02 crore = 28.86 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 10 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (10 − 6) ÷ (10 − 1) = 44.4

  2. Scorenot scored: not reported

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(0.0 + 10.0 + 44.4) ÷ 3 = 18.1
