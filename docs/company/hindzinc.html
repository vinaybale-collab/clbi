Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 47,82,041 + Scope 2 2,41,728 = 50,23,769 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity50,23,769 ÷ ₹40,844 crore = 123 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position8 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 8) ÷ (19 − 1) = 61.1

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 2,77,72,320 m³
  2. Intensity2,77,72,320 ÷ ₹40,844 crore = 680 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 7) ÷ (18 − 1) = 64.7

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 2,04,88,200 tonnes
  2. Intensity2,04,88,200 ÷ ₹40,844 crore = 501.6 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position13 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 13) ÷ (18 − 1) = 29.4

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 6,379 + SOx 22,252 + PM 1,240 = 29,871 tonnes
  2. Intensity29,871 ÷ ₹40,844 crore = 0.7313 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 12 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (12 − 7) ÷ (12 − 1) = 45.5

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score. Air rank: 7 of 12.

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(61.1 + 64.7 + 29.4) ÷ 3 = 51.7
  2. RestorationTier B restoration = +2.0
  3. Recycling68,07,723.09 recovered ÷ 2,04,88,200 generated = 0.332; 3 × 0.332 = +1.00
  4. Renewables2 × 0.646 (renewable share 64.6%) = +1.29

  7. RankRank 8 of 17 by final score in Metals & Mining; without credit it would rank 8.
