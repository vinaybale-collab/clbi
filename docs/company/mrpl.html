Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 52,62,173 + Scope 2 29,986 = 52,92,159 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity52,92,159 ÷ ₹1,05,155.49 crore = 50.33 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 11) ÷ (15 − 1) = 28.6

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 2,31,70,931 m³
  2. Intensity2,31,70,931 ÷ ₹1,05,155.49 crore = 220.3 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 12) ÷ (15 − 1) = 21.4

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 16,888.81 tonnes
  2. Intensity16,888.81 ÷ ₹1,05,155.49 crore = 0.1606 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position9 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 9) ÷ (14 − 1) = 38.5

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0
