Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 4,589 + Scope 2 12,701 = 17,290 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity17,290 ÷ ₹5,748 crore = 3.008 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position2 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 2) ÷ (26 − 1) = 96.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 50,571 m³
  2. Intensity50,571 ÷ ₹5,748 crore = 8.798 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position1 of 27 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (27 − 1) ÷ (27 − 1) = 100.0

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 6,664.42 tonnes
  2. Intensity6,664.42 ÷ ₹5,748 crore = 1.159 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 27 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (27 − 10) ÷ (27 − 1) = 65.4

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 0.426 + SOx 0.094 + PM 0.292 = 0.812 tonnes
  2. Intensitynot computed: revenue not matched to the BRSR year
  3. Scorenot scored: open data query (see below)

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score.

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(96.0 + 100.0 + 65.4) ÷ 3 = 87.1
  2. RestorationNo qualifying restoration = +0.0
  3. Recycling4,720 recovered ÷ 6,664.42 generated = 0.708; 3 × 0.708 = +2.12
  4. Renewables2 × 0.339 (renewable share 33.9%) = +0.68
  5. Credit+2.80 (capped at 10)
  6. Finalmin(100, 87.1 + 2.80) = 89.9
  7. RankRank 2 of 26 by final score in Chemicals; without credit it would rank 2.

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.12

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 70.8% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.68

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 33.9% renewable as reported.
