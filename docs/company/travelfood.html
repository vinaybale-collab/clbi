Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 1,320 + Scope 2 11,740 = 13,060 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity13,060 ÷ ₹1,302.14 crore = 10.03 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 20 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (20 − 11) ÷ (20 − 1) = 47.4

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 2,11,700 m³
  2. Intensity2,11,700 ÷ ₹1,302.14 crore = 162.6 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 23 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (23 − 15) ÷ (23 − 1) = 36.4

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 184.88 tonnes
  2. Intensity184.88 ÷ ₹1,302.14 crore = 0.142 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 20 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (20 − 6) ÷ (20 − 1) = 73.7

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+3.00

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 100.0% recovered as reported.
