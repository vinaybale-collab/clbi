Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 1,756.16 + Scope 2 26,761.37 = 28,517.53 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity28,517.53 ÷ ₹3,930.48 crore = 7.255 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position14 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 14) ÷ (19 − 1) = 27.8

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 62,123.31 m³
  2. Intensity62,123.31 ÷ ₹3,930.48 crore = 15.81 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 6) ÷ (19 − 1) = 72.2

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 1,419.89 tonnes
  2. Intensity1,419.89 ÷ ₹3,930.48 crore = 0.3613 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position4 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 4) ÷ (18 − 1) = 82.4

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+0.01

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 0.2% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.21

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 10.3% renewable as reported.
