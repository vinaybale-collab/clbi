Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 721 + Scope 2 10,631 = 11,352 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity11,352 ÷ ₹9,556.39 crore = 1.188 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position2 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 2) ÷ (11 − 1) = 90.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 2,82,542 m³
  2. Intensity2,82,542 ÷ ₹9,556.39 crore = 29.57 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 12 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (12 − 5) ÷ (12 − 1) = 63.6

  2. Intensity693 ÷ ₹9,556.39 crore = 0.07252 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 5) ÷ (11 − 1) = 60.0
