Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 2,04,30,000 + Scope 2 16,30,000 = 2,20,60,000 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity2,20,60,000 ÷ ₹8,86,224.41 crore = 24.89 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 7) ÷ (15 − 1) = 57.1

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 11,20,80,461 m³
  2. Intensity11,20,80,461 ÷ ₹8,86,224.41 crore = 126.5 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position9 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 9) ÷ (15 − 1) = 42.9

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 4,26,439 tonnes
  2. Intensity4,26,439 ÷ ₹8,86,224.41 crore = 0.4812 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 12) ÷ (14 − 1) = 15.4

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 19,813 + SOx 27,019 + PM 2,472 = 49,304 tonnes
  2. Intensity49,304 ÷ ₹8,86,224.41 crore = 0.05563 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 8 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (8 − 6) ÷ (8 − 1) = 28.6

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score.

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(57.1 + 42.9 + 15.4) ÷ 3 = 38.5
  2. RestorationTier B restoration = +2.0
  3. Recycling3,21,582 recovered ÷ 4,26,439 generated = 0.754; 3 × 0.754 = +2.26
