Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 69.3 + Scope 2 3,208 = 3,277.3 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity3,277.3 ÷ ₹3,757.42 crore = 0.8722 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 11) ÷ (19 − 1) = 44.4

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 67,753 m³
  2. Intensity67,753 ÷ ₹3,757.42 crore = 18.03 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position14 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 14) ÷ (18 − 1) = 23.5

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 88.94 tonnes
  2. Intensity88.94 ÷ ₹3,757.42 crore = 0.02367 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 10) ÷ (18 − 1) = 47.1

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 0 + SOx 0 + PM 0 = 0 tonnes
  2. Intensitynot computed: revenue not matched to the BRSR year
  3. Scorenot scored: reported zero, to be confirmed by the company

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+0.40

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 13.3% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+1.33

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 66.6% renewable as reported.
