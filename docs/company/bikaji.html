Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 32,546.44 + Scope 2 16,452.94 = 48,999.38 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity48,999.38 ÷ ₹2,817.18 crore = 17.39 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position21 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 21) ÷ (26 − 1) = 20.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 1,59,329 m³
  2. Intensity1,59,329 ÷ ₹2,817.18 crore = 56.56 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position13 of 29 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (29 − 13) ÷ (29 − 1) = 57.1

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 5,743.94 tonnes
  2. Intensity5,743.94 ÷ ₹2,817.18 crore = 2.039 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position19 of 29 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (29 − 19) ÷ (29 − 1) = 35.7

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 47.65 + SOx 42.87 + PM 91.94 = 182.46 tonnes
  2. Intensity182.46 ÷ ₹2,817.18 crore = 0.06477 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position21 of 23 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (23 − 21) ÷ (23 − 1) = 9.1

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0
