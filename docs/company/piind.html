Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 37,331 + Scope 2 1,14,401 = 1,51,732 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,51,732 ÷ ₹6,182.7 crore = 24.54 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position9 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 9) ÷ (26 − 1) = 68.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 8,28,624 m³
  2. Intensity8,28,624 ÷ ₹6,182.7 crore = 134 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 27 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (27 − 7) ÷ (27 − 1) = 76.9

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 1,32,847.86 tonnes
  2. Intensity1,32,847.86 ÷ ₹6,182.7 crore = 21.49 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position19 of 27 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (27 − 19) ÷ (27 − 1) = 30.8

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 70.77 + SOx 33.04 + PM 57.76 = 161.57 tonnes
  2. Intensity161.57 ÷ ₹6,182.7 crore = 0.02613 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 21 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (21 − 7) ÷ (21 − 1) = 70.0

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score. Air rank: 7 of 21.

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.74

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 91.3% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.20

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 10.1% renewable as reported.
