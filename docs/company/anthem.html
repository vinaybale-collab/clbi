How the score is calculated

Final score = average of the pressure scores. Each pressure score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where position ranks the company’s net intensity among the n peers with a scored value (1 = lowest), and net intensity = (gross − offset) ÷ revenue.

Net pressureNp = Gp − Op(1)
Net intensityIp = Gp − OpR(2)
Pressure scoresp = 100 × np − rpnp − 1(3)
Final scoreS = 14 Σp sp(4)

Final score = (69.2 + 41.0 + 30.8 + 2.6) ÷ 4 = 35.9; the 30th highest of 40 final scores in Healthcare: pharmaceuticals, so peer rank 30.

Illustrative We multiply each reported net quantity by a published damage price (central value, 2025 rupees). Read the result as an order of magnitude for each pressure. The figure estimates a cost to society. It is not a liability or a valuation, and no rank uses it. Do not add values across companies: one company’s Scope 2 is another’s Scope 1.
