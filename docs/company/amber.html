Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 8,676.54 + Scope 2 66,735.25 = 75,411.79 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity75,411.79 ÷ ₹12,186.48 crore = 6.188 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position13 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 13) ÷ (19 − 1) = 33.3

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 3,79,019.24 m³
  2. Intensity3,79,019.24 ÷ ₹12,186.48 crore = 31.1 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 12) ÷ (19 − 1) = 38.9

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 27,703.98 tonnes
  2. Intensity27,703.98 ÷ ₹12,186.48 crore = 2.273 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 15) ÷ (18 − 1) = 17.6

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 4.4 + SOx 2.33 + PM 10.9 = 17.63 tonnes
  2. Intensity17.63 ÷ ₹12,186.48 crore = 0.001447 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 9 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (9 − 6) ÷ (9 − 1) = 37.5

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score.

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(33.3 + 38.9 + 17.6) ÷ 3 = 30.0
  2. RestorationNo qualifying restoration = +0.0
  3. Recycling27,630.15 recovered ÷ 27,703.98 generated = 0.997; 3 × 0.997 = +2.99
  4. Renewables2 × 0.223 (renewable share 22.3%) = +0.45
  5. Credit+3.44 (capped at 10)
  6. Finalmin(100, 30.0 + 3.44) = 33.4
  7. RankRank 14 of 18 by final score in Consumer Durables; without credit it would rank 15.

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.99

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 99.7% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.45

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 22.3% renewable as reported.
