Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

  3. Position2 of 35 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (35 − 2) ÷ (35 − 1) = 97.1

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. A project was reviewed (Central Asian Flyway wetland prioritisation, Assessment not completed restoration) but did not qualify. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.18

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 72.7% recovered as reported.
