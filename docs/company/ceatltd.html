Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 1,14,858 + Scope 2 1,90,550 = 3,05,408 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity3,05,408 ÷ ₹15,214.86 crore = 20.07 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position27 of 35 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (35 − 27) ÷ (35 − 1) = 23.5

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 13,53,062 m³
  2. Intensity13,53,062 ÷ ₹15,214.86 crore = 88.93 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position27 of 36 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (36 − 27) ÷ (36 − 1) = 25.7

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 27,125.28 tonnes
  2. Intensity27,125.28 ÷ ₹15,214.86 crore = 1.783 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position16 of 36 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (36 − 16) ÷ (36 − 1) = 57.1

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 26 + SOx 69 + PM 308 = 403 tonnes
  2. Intensity403 ÷ ₹15,214.86 crore = 0.02649 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position20 of 24 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (24 − 20) ÷ (24 − 1) = 17.4

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.57

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 85.7% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.62

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 30.9% renewable as reported.
