Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 1,00,136 + Scope 2 18,639 = 1,18,775 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,18,775 ÷ ₹9,982.72 crore = 11.9 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position9 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 9) ÷ (11 − 1) = 20.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 26,30,961.33 m³
  2. Intensity26,30,961.33 ÷ ₹9,982.72 crore = 263.6 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 10) ÷ (11 − 1) = 10.0

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 13,776.36 tonnes

  3. Position3 of 8 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (8 − 3) ÷ (8 − 1) = 71.4
