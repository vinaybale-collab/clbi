Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 2,819.23 + Scope 2 24,479.38 = 27,298.61 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity27,298.61 ÷ ₹1,949.01 crore = 14.01 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position38 of 56 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (56 − 38) ÷ (56 − 1) = 32.7

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 54,125 m³
  2. Intensity54,125 ÷ ₹1,949.01 crore = 27.77 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position22 of 61 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (61 − 22) ÷ (61 − 1) = 65.0

  3. Position28 of 61 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (61 − 28) ÷ (61 − 1) = 55.0

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+0.23

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 7.5% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.15

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 7.6% renewable as reported.
