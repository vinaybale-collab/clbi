Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 12,227.58 + Scope 2 40,118.88 = 52,346.46 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity52,346.46 ÷ ₹2,216.05 crore = 23.62 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position24 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 24) ÷ (26 − 1) = 8.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 8,47,471 m³
  2. Intensity8,47,471 ÷ ₹2,216.05 crore = 382.4 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position27 of 29 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (29 − 27) ÷ (29 − 1) = 7.1

  3. Position27 of 29 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (29 − 27) ÷ (29 − 1) = 7.1

  3. Recycling34,324.43 recovered ÷ 35,989.588 generated = 0.954; 3 × 0.954 = +2.86
  4. Renewables2 × 0.058 (renewable share 5.8%) = +0.12

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.86

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 95.4% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.12

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 5.8% renewable as reported.
