Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 3,16,42,403.73 + Scope 2 5,903.04 = 3,16,48,306.77 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity3,16,48,306.77 ÷ ₹17,489.53 crore = 1,810 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position13 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 13) ÷ (15 − 1) = 14.3

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 25,29,62,099.88 m³
  2. Intensity25,29,62,099.88 ÷ ₹17,489.53 crore = 14,464 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 12) ÷ (15 − 1) = 21.4

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 45,64,866.829 tonnes
  2. Intensity45,64,866.829 ÷ ₹17,489.53 crore = 261 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 10) ÷ (14 − 1) = 30.8
