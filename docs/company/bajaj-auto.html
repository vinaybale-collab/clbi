Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 26,483.7 + Scope 2 81,604.1 = 1,08,087.8 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,08,087.8 ÷ ₹58,732.48 crore = 1.84 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 35 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (35 − 5) ÷ (35 − 1) = 88.2

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 10,97,550.9 m³
  2. Intensity10,97,550.9 ÷ ₹58,732.48 crore = 18.69 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 36 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (36 − 6) ÷ (36 − 1) = 85.7

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 14,680.5 tonnes
  2. Intensity14,680.5 ÷ ₹58,732.48 crore = 0.25 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position1 of 36 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (36 − 1) ÷ (36 − 1) = 100.0

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 6.9 + SOx 8.3 + PM 130.1 = 145.3 tonnes
  2. Intensity145.3 ÷ ₹58,732.48 crore = 0.002474 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 24 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.45
