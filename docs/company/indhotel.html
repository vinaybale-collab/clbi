Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 20,301.4 + Scope 2 47,312.65 = 67,614.05 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity67,614.05 ÷ ₹5,379.55 crore = 12.57 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position14 of 20 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (20 − 14) ÷ (20 − 1) = 31.6

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 22,69,612.91 m³
  2. Intensity22,69,612.91 ÷ ₹5,379.55 crore = 421.9 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position22 of 23 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (23 − 22) ÷ (23 − 1) = 4.5

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 8,283.28 tonnes
  2. Intensity8,283.28 ÷ ₹5,379.55 crore = 1.54 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 20 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (20 − 15) ÷ (20 − 1) = 26.3

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.41

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 80.2% recovered as reported.
