Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 5,138 + Scope 2 2,034 = 7,172 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity7,172 ÷ ₹6,321.85 crore = 1.134 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position12 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 12) ÷ (19 − 1) = 38.9

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 21,676 m³
  2. Intensity21,676 ÷ ₹6,321.85 crore = 3.429 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 3) ÷ (18 − 1) = 88.2

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 143 tonnes
  2. Intensity143 ÷ ₹6,321.85 crore = 0.02262 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position8 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 8) ÷ (18 − 1) = 58.8

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 1.336 + SOx 0.00016 + PM 0.148 = 1.484 tonnes
  2. Intensity1.484 ÷ ₹6,321.85 crore = 0.0002348 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 11) ÷ (11 − 1) = 0.0

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0
