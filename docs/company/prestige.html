Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 8,865.12 + Scope 2 75,781.91 = 84,647.03 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity84,647.03 ÷ ₹12,685.4 crore = 6.673 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 9 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (9 − 6) ÷ (9 − 1) = 37.5

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 7,08,813.78 m³
  2. Intensity7,08,813.78 ÷ ₹12,685.4 crore = 55.88 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 3) ÷ (11 − 1) = 80.0

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 4,736.14 tonnes
  2. Intensity4,736.14 ÷ ₹12,685.4 crore = 0.3734 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position1 of 10 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (10 − 1) ÷ (10 − 1) = 100.0

  2. Scorenot scored: not reported

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(37.5 + 80.0 + 100.0) ÷ 3 = 72.5
  2. RestorationNo qualifying restoration = +0.0
  3. Recycling2,545.19 recovered ÷ 4,736.14 generated = 0.537; 3 × 0.537 = +1.61

  6. Finalmin(100, 72.5 + 2.42) = 74.9

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+1.61

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 53.7% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.80

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 40.2% renewable as reported.
