Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 30,68,890 + Scope 2 4,40,977 = 35,09,867 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity35,09,867 ÷ ₹1,38,696.52 crore = 25.31 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position8 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 8) ÷ (15 − 1) = 50.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 2,00,18,355 m³
  2. Intensity2,00,18,355 ÷ ₹1,38,696.52 crore = 144.3 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 11) ÷ (15 − 1) = 28.6

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 13,108.1 tonnes
  2. Intensity13,108.1 ÷ ₹1,38,696.52 crore = 0.09451 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 6) ÷ (14 − 1) = 61.5

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 645 + SOx 299 + PM 156 = 1,100 tonnes
  2. Intensity1,100 ÷ ₹1,38,696.52 crore = 0.007931 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position1 of 8 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (8 − 1) ÷ (8 − 1) = 100.0

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score.

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(50.0 + 28.6 + 61.5) ÷ 3 = 46.7
  2. RestorationTier B restoration = +2.0
  3. Recycling12,171.19 recovered ÷ 13,108.1 generated = 0.929; 3 × 0.929 = +2.79
  4. Renewables2 × 0.080 (renewable share 8.0%) = +0.16
