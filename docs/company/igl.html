Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 4,70,082.95 + Scope 2 1,54,131.79 = 6,24,214.74 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity6,24,214.74 ÷ ₹17,845.71 crore = 34.98 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 10) ÷ (15 − 1) = 35.7

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 7,944 m³
  2. Intensity7,944 ÷ ₹17,845.71 crore = 0.4451 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position1 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 1) ÷ (15 − 1) = 100.0

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 364.51 tonnes
  2. Intensity364.51 ÷ ₹17,845.71 crore = 0.02043 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position4 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 4) ÷ (14 − 1) = 76.9

Air

  2. Intensitynot computed: revenue not matched to the BRSR year

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+3.00

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 100.0% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.01

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 0.3% renewable as reported.
