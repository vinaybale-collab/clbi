Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 182.91 + Scope 2 19,400.06 = 19,582.97 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity19,582.97 ÷ ₹9,207.1 crore = 2.127 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position18 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 18) ÷ (19 − 1) = 5.6

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 2,20,749 m³
  2. Intensity2,20,749 ÷ ₹9,207.1 crore = 23.98 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position16 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 16) ÷ (18 − 1) = 11.8

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 236.89 tonnes
  2. Intensity236.89 ÷ ₹9,207.1 crore = 0.02573 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 11) ÷ (18 − 1) = 41.2

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 0.073 + SOx 0.023 + PM 0.018 = 0.114 tonnes
  2. Intensity0.114 ÷ ₹9,207.1 crore = 1.241 × 10-5 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 5) ÷ (11 − 1) = 60.0

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. A project was reviewed (Integrated environment restoration village work, Qualitative restoration project) but did not qualify. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.92

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 97.2% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.75

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 37.4% renewable as reported.
