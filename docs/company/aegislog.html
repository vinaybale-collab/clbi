Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 46 + Scope 2 5,356 = 5,402 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity5,402 ÷ ₹4,491.54 crore = 1.203 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position1 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 1) ÷ (15 − 1) = 100.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 13,183 m³
  2. Intensity13,183 ÷ ₹4,491.54 crore = 2.935 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 3) ÷ (15 − 1) = 85.7

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 17.72 tonnes
  2. Intensity17.72 ÷ ₹4,491.54 crore = 0.003945 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position2 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 2) ÷ (14 − 1) = 92.3

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+0.46

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 15.4% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.52
