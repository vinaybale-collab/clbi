Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 24,160 + Scope 2 77,207 = 1,01,367 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,01,367 ÷ ₹5,542.06 crore = 18.29 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position23 of 35 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (35 − 23) ÷ (35 − 1) = 35.3

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 6,54,163 m³
  2. Intensity6,54,163 ÷ ₹5,542.06 crore = 118 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position33 of 36 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (36 − 33) ÷ (36 − 1) = 8.6

  3. Position30 of 36 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (36 − 30) ÷ (36 − 1) = 17.1

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.
