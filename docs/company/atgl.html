Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 91,721 + Scope 2 47,991 = 1,39,712 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,39,712 ÷ ₹6,408.53 crore = 21.8 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 6) ÷ (15 − 1) = 64.3

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 1,24,586 m³
  2. Intensity1,24,586 ÷ ₹6,408.53 crore = 19.44 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position5 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 5) ÷ (15 − 1) = 71.4

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 127.35 tonnes
  2. Intensity127.35 ÷ ₹6,408.53 crore = 0.01987 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 3) ÷ (14 − 1) = 84.6
