Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 5,868.43 + Scope 2 1,21,963.58 = 1,27,832.01 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,27,832.01 ÷ ₹77,441.29 crore = 1.651 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position20 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 20) ÷ (26 − 1) = 24.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 1,24,965 m³
  2. Intensity1,24,965 ÷ ₹77,441.29 crore = 1.614 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position2 of 25 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (25 − 2) ÷ (25 − 1) = 95.8

Waste

  2. Intensity791.18 ÷ ₹77,441.29 crore = 0.01022 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position19 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 19) ÷ (26 − 1) = 28.0

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+1.70

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 56.8% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.02

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 1.0% renewable as reported.
