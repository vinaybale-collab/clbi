Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 14,81,280 + Scope 2 7,365 = 14,88,645 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity14,88,645 ÷ ₹20,793.66 crore = 71.59 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position13 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 13) ÷ (26 − 1) = 52.0

  4. Score100 × (27 − 20) ÷ (27 − 1) = 26.9

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. A project was reviewed (Crop residue burning prevention, Annual sustainable agriculture programme) but did not qualify. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+2.57

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 85.6% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.26

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 12.8% renewable as reported.
