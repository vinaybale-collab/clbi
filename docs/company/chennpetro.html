Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 25,76,948 + Scope 2 53,432 = 26,30,380 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity26,30,380 ÷ ₹78,610.66 crore = 33.46 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position9 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 9) ÷ (15 − 1) = 42.9

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 84,15,670 m³
  2. Intensity84,15,670 ÷ ₹78,610.66 crore = 107.1 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position7 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 7) ÷ (15 − 1) = 57.1

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 19,202.94 tonnes
  2. Intensity19,202.94 ÷ ₹78,610.66 crore = 0.2443 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 10) ÷ (14 − 1) = 30.8

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 618.17 + SOx 1,339.49 + PM 412.9 = 2,370.56 tonnes
  2. Intensity2,370.56 ÷ ₹78,610.66 crore = 0.03016 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position4 of 8 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (8 − 4) ÷ (8 − 1) = 57.1

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score.

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(42.9 + 57.1 + 30.8) ÷ 3 = 43.6
  2. RestorationTier B restoration = +2.0
  3. Recycling10,010.57 recovered ÷ 19,202.94 generated = 0.521; 3 × 0.521 = +1.56
  4. Renewables2 × 0.007 (renewable share 0.7%) = +0.01
  5. Credit+3.58 (capped at 10)
  6. Finalmin(100, 43.6 + 3.58) = 47.2
  7. RankRank 9 of 14 by final score in Oil Gas & Consumable Fuels; without credit it would rank 9.
