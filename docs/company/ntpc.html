Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 30,90,07,966.99 + Scope 2 72,361.35 = 30,90,80,328.34 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity30,90,80,328.34 ÷ ₹1,65,493.74 crore = 1,868 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position14 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 14) ÷ (15 − 1) = 7.1

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 6,03,53,72,116.3 m³
  2. Intensity6,03,53,72,116.3 ÷ ₹1,65,493.74 crore = 36,469 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position14 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 14) ÷ (15 − 1) = 7.1

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 57,192.8 tonnes
  2. Intensity57,192.8 ÷ ₹1,65,493.74 crore = 0.3456 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 14 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (14 − 6) ÷ (14 − 1) = 61.5

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 5,38,969.99 + SOx 12,47,429.06 + PM 65,081.33 = 18,51,480.38 tonnes
  2. Intensity18,51,480.38 ÷ ₹1,65,493.74 crore = 11.19 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 8 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (8 − 6) ÷ (8 − 1) = 28.6

Air is ranked separately and is not part of the pressure score.

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(7.1 + 7.1 + 61.5) ÷ 3 = 25.3
  2. RestorationTier B restoration = +2.0
  3. Recycling53,646.29 recovered ÷ 57,192.8 generated = 0.938; 3 × 0.938 = +2.81
  4. Renewables2 × 0.057 (renewable share 5.7%) = +0.11
  5. Credit+4.93 (capped at 10)
  6. Finalmin(100, 25.3 + 4.93) = 30.2
  7. RankRank 10 of 13 by final score in Power; without credit it would rank 10.
