Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 745 + Scope 2 4,604 = 5,349 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity5,349 ÷ ₹53,124.64 crore = 0.1007 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position2 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 2) ÷ (11 − 1) = 90.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 1,55,101 m³
  2. Intensity1,55,101 ÷ ₹53,124.64 crore = 2.92 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position6 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 6) ÷ (11 − 1) = 50.0

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 18.85 tonnes
  2. Intensity18.85 ÷ ₹53,124.64 crore = 0.0003548 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position2 of 9 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (9 − 2) ÷ (9 − 1) = 87.5

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+3.00

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 100.0% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.82

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 40.8% renewable as reported.
