Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 2,31,662.4 + Scope 2 1,64,510.32 = 3,96,172.72 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity3,96,172.72 ÷ ₹43,494.91 crore = 9.108 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 3) ÷ (15 − 1) = 85.7

Water

  2. Intensity1,03,669.2 ÷ ₹43,494.91 crore = 2.383 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position2 of 15 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (15 − 2) ÷ (15 − 1) = 92.9

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. A project was reviewed (Kochi mangrove conservation and restoration, Qualitative restoration with assessment) but did not qualify. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+1.99

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 66.3% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.01

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 0.7% renewable as reported.
