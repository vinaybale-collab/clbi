Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 8,10,210.573 + Scope 2 764.028 = 8,10,974.601 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity8,10,974.601 ÷ ₹3,659.04 crore = 221.6 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position11 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 11) ÷ (11 − 1) = 0.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 1,65,222.78 m³
  2. Intensity1,65,222.78 ÷ ₹3,659.04 crore = 45.15 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position8 of 12 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (12 − 8) ÷ (12 − 1) = 36.4

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 5,556.65 tonnes
  2. Intensity5,556.65 ÷ ₹3,659.04 crore = 1.519 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 10) ÷ (11 − 1) = 10.0

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 16,349.74 + SOx 1,728.25 + PM 929.47 = 19,007.46 tonnes
  2. Intensity19,007.46 ÷ ₹3,659.04 crore = 5.195 tonnes per ₹ crore

From element scores to rank

  1. Pressure(0.0 + 36.4 + 10.0) ÷ 3 = 15.5
  2. RestorationNo qualifying restoration = +0.0
  3. Recycling73.56 recovered ÷ 5,556.65 generated = 0.013; 3 × 0.013 = +0.04

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+0.04

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 1.3% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.44

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 21.8% renewable as reported.
