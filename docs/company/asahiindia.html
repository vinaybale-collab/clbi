Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

  1. QuantityScope 1 3,24,831 + Scope 2 1,59,026 = 4,83,857 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity4,83,857 ÷ ₹4,676.24 crore = 103.5 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position35 of 35 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (35 − 35) ÷ (35 − 1) = 0.0

  4. Score100 × (36 − 34) ÷ (36 − 1) = 5.7

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0
