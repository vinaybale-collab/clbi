Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 92,214 + Scope 2 18,202 = 1,10,416 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity1,10,416 ÷ ₹20,976.39 crore = 5.264 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position10 of 26 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (26 − 10) ÷ (26 − 1) = 64.0

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 13,43,009 m³
  2. Intensity13,43,009 ÷ ₹20,976.39 crore = 64.02 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 29 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (29 − 15) ÷ (29 − 1) = 50.0

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 56,562.89 tonnes
  2. Intensity56,562.89 ÷ ₹20,976.39 crore = 2.697 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position23 of 29 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (29 − 23) ÷ (29 − 1) = 21.4
