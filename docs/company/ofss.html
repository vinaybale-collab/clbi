Calculation, step by step

Worked with this company’s own numbers. Intensity = value ÷ revenue. Element score = 100 × (n − position) ÷ (n − 1), where n is the number of peers with a scored value and position 1 is the lowest intensity. Each reproduced score is checked against the published one.

Climate

  1. QuantityScope 1 453 + Scope 2 6,782 = 7,235 tCO₂e
  2. Intensity7,235 ÷ ₹5,716.7 crore = 1.266 tCO₂e per ₹ crore
  3. Position15 of 19 companies in the peer group with a scored climate value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (19 − 15) ÷ (19 − 1) = 22.2

Water

  1. QuantityWater withdrawal = 29,148 m³
  2. Intensity29,148 ÷ ₹5,716.7 crore = 5.099 m³ per ₹ crore
  3. Position4 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored water value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 4) ÷ (18 − 1) = 82.4

Waste

  1. QuantityWaste generated = 86.05 tonnes
  2. Intensity86.05 ÷ ₹5,716.7 crore = 0.01505 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position3 of 18 companies in the peer group with a scored waste value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (18 − 3) ÷ (18 − 1) = 88.2

Air

  1. QuantityNOx 0.66 + SOx 0.06 + PM 0.03 = 0.75 tonnes
  2. Intensity0.75 ÷ ₹5,716.7 crore = 0.0001312 tonnes per ₹ crore
  3. Position8 of 11 companies in the peer group with a scored air value, lowest intensity first
  4. Score100 × (11 − 8) ÷ (11 − 1) = 30.0

Credit for positive action

Up to 10 points in total: restoration (+5 for Tier A, +2 for Tier B), recycling (0–3) and renewable electricity (0–2). Credit recognises effort; it does not offset pressure.

Restoration+0.0

No quantified restoration in FY2025-26 disclosures met the rule. Plans, commitments, unfinished work and narrative claims earn no credit.

Recycling+3.00

3 × (waste recovered ÷ waste generated), with 100.0% recovered as reported.

Renewable electricity+0.47

2 × renewable share of electricity, with 23.4% renewable as reported.
