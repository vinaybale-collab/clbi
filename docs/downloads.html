Corporate Nature Pressure Index (CNPI), v1.2

Reported pressure on nature by India’s 500 largest listed companies, compared within peer groups.

Urvara.Life with Landstack, prepared for ILDC 2026. Draft, not for publication.

Data: Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reports for FY2025-26 (1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026), annual financial filings and company reports. Every value is the company’s own disclosure unless marked otherwise.
