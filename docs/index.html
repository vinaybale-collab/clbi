How much pressure do India’s 500 largest companies put on nature for each rupee they earn?

We take the climate, energy, water and pollution figures each company reports, subtract the offsets it reports, and compare it with peers in the same business.

Where the comparison holds, and where it stops

Most companies report the four operational pressures and pass our checks. BRSR has no field for land, the pressure most tied to biodiversity.

Four pressures, plus air

We subtract the offset a company reports for each pressure and divide what remains by revenue.

What the data can and cannot support

  • Compare reported pressure per rupee within a line of business
  • Show how renewables, recycling and returned water cut pressure
  • Trace every number to its filing, field or page
  • Map what is not disclosed
  • Measure biodiversity impact or nature-related risk
  • Compare across sectors or absolute footprints
  • Cover supply chains or what banks finance
  • Show change over time: the data cover one year, FY2025-26
