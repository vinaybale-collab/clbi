01 · The question
For every rupee a company earns, how much pressure does it put on nature?
CLBI compares each company only with its own sector, on three pressures it reports itself: greenhouse gases, water and waste.
A rank means
Among its sector peers, the company reported the lowest pressure per rupee of revenue in FY2025-26, on climate, water and waste together.
A rank does not mean
· a small footprint in absolute terms
· a biodiversity-impact or risk score
· an ESG rating, or a ranking across sectors
· that any company has done something wrong
A cement maker is never compared with a bank. Every comparison is within the sector.

02 · Principles
Six rules we did not bend
01
Company data only
Ranks use only what companies themselves report. Nothing estimated or modelled enters a rank; a blank stays blank.
02
Every number traceable
Each value carries its link, page or filing field, and the exact quote when taken from a report.
03
Like with like
Ranked only within a sector, and only where at least 10 companies have complete data.
04
Simple, open maths
Anyone can reproduce every score; the dataset recomputes all 500 with spreadsheet formulas.
05
Doubtful values held back
A value that looks wrong is not scored until the company confirms it, even if it is printed.
06
Credit never offsets
Restoration, recycling and renewables earn a few points; they never cancel emissions or water use.
If a number cannot be traced to the company’s own disclosure, it does not enter a rank.

06 · Doubtful values
A printed number is not automatically a true number
Three rules hold a value back. It stays in the dataset, flagged, and is restored when the company confirms it.
29
Extreme values
More than 300× above or below the peer median, per ₹ crore. Usually kilograms entered as tonnes, or head-office-only boundaries. Climate 3 · water 3 · waste 13 · air 10.
2
Physically impossible
Scope 2 implying more grid electricity than the company’s whole revenue could buy. One printed Scope 2 is 147× the company’s own previous year.
30
Reported zeros
Companies with a zero climate, water or waste total, or a zero Scope 1 or Scope 2 alone, across the 500. For an operating company, zero usually means ‘not measured’.
A company with a held-back climate, water or waste value is not ranked until it is resolved.

15 · What would make CLBI stronger
Four disclosures would turn this into a full land and nature ranking
Ask 01
Land
Report total land owned and leased, in hectares, including mining leases. It unlocks a biodiversity rank for every company.
Ask 02
Units
Use the BRSR units exactly (tonnes, kilolitres). 31 values are held back, most of them likely unit or boundary slips.
Ask 03
Air
Report NOx, SOx and dust as tonnes per year, not only stack concentrations: half of companies cannot be compared today.
Ask 04
Scope 2
State the basis (location or market) and the gross figure before renewable certificates.
Companies can correct any value with a page reference to their own published report.
